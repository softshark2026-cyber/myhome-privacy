      My Home Privacy Policy

      Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Covers My Home for Android (and iOS, where available)

      My Home is a family organizer app: a shared calendar, shopping lists, chores, a family tree, chat, and games. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app handles, where it lives, and how to delete it.

      🏠 One app, shared with your family 📺 Shows ads by default 💳 Remove Ads available as a one-time purchase
      01

      The short version

      My Home connects your family's calendar, shopping lists, chores, and more across everyone's devices. Here's what matters most:

      • Your family's data is not stored on any server I run. Instead, your family connects the app to its own, separate backend project hosted by Supabase (a third-party database provider) that one family member sets up. I don't operate that project, can't log into it, and have no access to what your family stores there.
      • Ads are shown by default. You can remove them permanently with a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, processed by Google Play.
      • Before you create or join a family, the app briefly stores just your name and date of birth locally on your device — nothing is sent anywhere until you connect to (or start) a family.

      I (the person who builds My Home) do not run a central server that collects data from every family using the app. Each family's data lives in its own, separate database that only that family controls.

      02

      Who this policy covers

      This policy covers My Home, published solely by Mihael Šarko ("I", "me", "the developer") — an independent developer, not a company.

      03

      Information the app handles

      Once you create or join a family, the following kinds of information may be stored in your family's own Supabase project — not on a server I control:

      Account information

      • Email address and password (password is handled entirely by Supabase's authentication system; the app and its developer never see or store it in plain text)
      • First name, last name
      • Date of birth (required at setup — used to show birthday reminders in the family calendar)

      Content your family creates

      • Calendar events, shopping lists and items, and chores/tasks (including who they're assigned to)
      • Family tree entries: names, birth dates, gender, and notes for family members you add
      • Chat messages — both group and one-to-one — between members of your family
      • Polls and votes, recipes, pantry items, and school schedules you choose to enter
      • Photos you upload to your family's shared album or recipes
      • Multiplayer game invitations and in-progress game state (for the games tab)

      What I don't collect

      I don't run analytics or crash-reporting that sends your usage data to me. I don't see your family's calendar, chores, messages, or photos — they live only in your family's own Supabase project.

      04

      Before you connect to a family

      The first time you open the app, before you've created or joined a family, it works locally: it asks for your first name, last name, and date of birth (all required), and stores that on your device only, using a local database (SQLite). Nothing is sent to the internet at this stage. Once you create a new family or join one with an invite code, your profile and content start syncing to that family's Supabase project as described in Section 5.

      If you never connect to a family, or use the in-app "Erase All Data" option before doing so, this locally-stored information is simply erased — I never had a copy of it.

      05

      How your family's data is actually stored

      My Home uses a "bring your own backend" model. In practice, that means:

      1. One family member creates a free Supabase project (at supabase.com) and runs a setup script I provide inside that project.
      2. They enter that project's URL and a public API key into the app.
      3. Other family members join using an invite code or link, generated from inside that same project.
      4. From then on, the app talks directly to that family's own Supabase project — never to a server I operate.

      This means your family effectively acts as its own data controller for its Supabase project, and Supabase, Inc. acts as the underlying hosting/processing provider. You can read Supabase's own privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy. Access within a family's project is restricted to accounts created with that family's invite code — a stranger cannot join without one.

      06

      Advertising

      My Home shows ads by default. You can remove them permanently with a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase from inside the app.

      Ads are served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers and general location (derived from IP address) to serve and measure ads. For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (via the User Messaging Platform) when required, so you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising.

      You can review how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/privacy and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

      07

      In-app purchases

      The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. I receive only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded — I never see your payment card or billing details.

      09

      How long information is kept

      • Before connecting to a family: until you delete it yourself, or uninstall the app.
      • After connecting: for as long as your family's Supabase project exists and your account within it hasn't been deleted.
      10

      Deleting your account and data

      You're always in control of your own data, and deletion is permanent — there is no way for me to undo it, because I don't hold a backup copy outside your own device or your family's own Supabase project.

      From inside the app

      Go to Settings and tap:

      • "Delete My Account" (once you've connected to a family) — permanently deletes your account and everything you personally created (your events, chores, messages, photos, recipes, etc.). Content that other family members created, or that was simply assigned to you, stays for the rest of the family — only the link to your account is removed.
      • "Erase All Data" (before you've connected to a family) — permanently erases everything stored on this device and returns the app to its first-run state.

      If your family runs its own Supabase project and hasn't applied the latest setup script yet, account deletion may not work until a family member re-runs it from Settings. The in-app flow will tell you if this happens.

      If you can't access the app

      I cannot delete your data for you. Each family's data lives in a Supabase project that only that family created and controls. I never receive, see, or store the login credentials or API keys for any family's project — so I have no technical way to open it, read it, or delete anything inside it myself, no matter who asks me.

      If you can't sign in to the app yourself, there are only two ways to remove your account:

      1. Ask the family member who set up your family's Supabase project to delete your account directly from their own Supabase dashboard (Authentication → Users) — they're the only one with access to do this.
      2. If you still have the app and can sign in, use "Delete My Account" in Settings yourself.

      If neither is possible — for example, you've lost contact with the family member who administers that project — I genuinely have no fallback to offer. I don't hold a copy of your data anywhere I could delete it from.

      11

      Children's privacy

      My Home is designed to be managed by an adult family member, who may choose to add information about other family members — including children — such as names and birth dates, to the family tree or calendar. The app is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

      If you believe a child has created their own account without a parent's involvement, I can't remove it myself — I have no access to any family's Supabase project or the accounts inside it (see Section 10). The account must be deleted either by the family member who administers that family's Supabase project (from their own dashboard), or by signing in and using "Delete My Account" directly.

      12

      Security

      Data sent between the app and your family's Supabase project is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to a family's data is restricted to accounts created through that family's own invite flow, enforced by database-level access rules I configure as part of the setup script. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but I design the app so that a stranger with the app installed cannot read another family's data without that family's invite code.

      13

      Your rights

      Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. In this app, you can exercise most of these yourself at any time:

      • Access & correction: open any screen in the app — your profile, events, messages, etc. — and edit it directly.
      • Deletion: use the in-app "Delete My Account" / "Erase All Data" option described in Section 10.
      • Portability: copy out what you need directly from the app. I can't export or transfer your family's data on your behalf, because I have no access to any family's Supabase project — only the family member who administers it can pull a full export, from their own Supabase dashboard.

      Questions about any of this are welcome at soft.shark2026@gmail.com — but please note I can only answer questions, not act on your family's data myself (see Section 10).
